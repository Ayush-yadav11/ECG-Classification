    Can the classifier diagnose a heart condition?

    No. It is a study project on a public dataset, not a medical device, and it should not inform anyone’s care.

    Where do the heartbeats come from?

    The MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database on PhysioNet, cut into single beats by Kachuee and colleagues in 2018 and shared on Kaggle.

    Why is the classifier’s fusion precision low?

    Training has only 641 fusion beats, resampled to 20,000. The network finds 91% of them but also calls 437 normal beats fusion.

    Does the ECG notebook run on Colab?

    Open the notebook in Colab and run every cell; it downloads the data itself. On a laptop CPU it trained in about 25 minutes.

    What changed in the classifier?

    The noise went from σ 0.5 to σ 0.05, early stopping now watches a validation split, and the chart labels follow N, S, V, F, Q.
